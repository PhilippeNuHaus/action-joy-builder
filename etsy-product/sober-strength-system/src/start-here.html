Your First Steps

01
Read pages 2 and 5

Page 2 is the safety notice and it matters. Page 5 explains the recovery curve the whole program is built on. Together they take about six minutes.

02
Write your trigger window on page 8

The time your first drink usually happened. There is a blank line for it. This single answer determines when you train.

03
Put three sessions in your calendar this week

Phase 01, Sessions A, B and C, from pages 12 to 14. Inside or just before your trigger window, with a rest day between each.

04
Do the check-in tomorrow morning

Sixty seconds, page 7. Score your sleep and your craving, and let the score pick the session. Do not skip this on day one — it is the habit the whole system rests on.

05
Write today's day number in the log

Whatever it is. Day 1 counts. Day 200 counts. Write it down.

Before you start — please read page 2

If you drink heavily or daily, stopping suddenly without medical supervision can be dangerous and in some cases fatal. This program is a training plan, not a detox plan or a treatment plan. Speak to a doctor first. In the US, SAMHSA's National Helpline is 1-800-662-4357 — free, confidential, 24 hours a day.
